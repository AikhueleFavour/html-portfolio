Favour Recipe For The Best Cinnamon Rolls

Ingredients


For the Dough :
  • 3/4 cup of warm water
  • 1/4 of cup butter
  • 3 cups breads flour
  • 1 egg plus 1 yolk

For the Filling :
  • 2/3 cup of brown sugar
  • 1/4 cup of butter
  • 1 1/2 tablespoon ground cinnamon

INSTRUCTIONS
  1. Mix the milk and the yeast
  2. Add the remaining and mix until it is dough
  3. After the dough has doubled in size roll it out in rentangular shape
